About Us

What drives us

Our Mission

To empower young people in Kirehe District, particularly unemployed youth and emerging creatives, by helping them discover their talents and transform their potential into sustainable sources of income. Through arts, design and cultural entrepreneurship, we provide access to practical skills development, mentorship, financial guidance and market opportunities.

We stand out by connecting talent discovery with the resources, guidance and opportunities young people need to turn their creativity into meaningful livelihoods and long-term economic independence. Our aim is to reduce the unemployment rate from 32.8% to 20% by 2030.

Our Vision

A Rwanda where every young person sees their talent as a real opportunity, and where graduates create jobs instead of waiting for them.

What we believe

  • Talent is a resource.Every young person has a talent that can become a source of income.
  • Creativity can be a livelihood.Arts, design and cultural entrepreneurship can lead to long-term economic independence.
  • We do hard things.Our team motto: we take on the hard work of turning ideas into results.

Problem Statement

Graduates of universities and high schools in Rwanda are unemployed more often than people with no formal education, so education alone is not producing economic development. Many wait for jobs that match their skills, or do not know about the opportunities that exist, which leaves talent and resources underused. Our work begins in Kirehe District.

Why It Persists

Opportunities are weakly connected to the training, mentorship and financial support that could help youth start businesses.

What Youth Told Us

In five in-person interviews in Kigali, we heard about fear of failure, limited capital, little information and difficulty finding customers.

Our Solution

Youth Empowerment Towards Economic Development

A digital platform that connects talent discovery with the skills, guidance and opportunities young people in Kirehe District need to turn their creativity into a livelihood.

Talent Discovery

Helping young people find what they are good at, in arts, design and culture.

Practical Skills

Hands-on training that turns a talent into work people will pay for.

Mentorship

Guidance from young entrepreneurs, artists and small business owners.

Financial Guidance

Loan options, business plans built on what youth already have, and saving early.

Market Opportunities

Jobs, internships and a place to showcase products and services to investors and partners.

How We Reach Youth

Through schools, social media and community meetings.
