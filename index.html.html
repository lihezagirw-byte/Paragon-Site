Turning talent into jobs for Rwanda’s youth

We are five African Leadership University students working on youth unemployment, helping educated young people turn their skills in arts, design and culture into income.

About Us

Who we are and what drives us

Our Mission

By 2029, we aim to reduce the unemployment rate of educated people from 35% to 20% by reducing dependency on job hunting. We inspire youth, especially students, to work hard on their talents, because talent can become a source of income, jobs and investment in areas like tourism and cultural preservation.

Our vision

A Rwanda where every young person sees their talent as a real opportunity, and where graduates create jobs instead of waiting for them.

The Problem

35%

of university graduates are unemployed (NISR 2021)

30%

of high school graduates are unemployed (NISR 2021)

24.5%

of young people were not in employment, education or training in 2025

Problem Statement

University and high school graduates have higher unemployment rates than uneducated people (22%) in Rwanda, so education alone is not producing economic development. Many graduates wait for jobs that match their skills, or do not know about the opportunities that exist, which leaves resources underused. Strengthening arts, design and culture as a way to invest in talent could significantly reduce unemployment by 2029.

What we found

Why It Persists

Opportunities are weakly connected to the training, mentorship and financial support that could help youth start businesses.

What Youth Told Us

In five in-person interviews in Kigali, we heard about fear of failure, limited capital, little information and difficulty finding customers.

Our Solution

A digital platform connecting youth to jobs, financial literacy, mentorship and markets, shared through schools, social media and community meetings.
